# Product backlog

For task selection and statuses, read the compact [INDEX.md](INDEX.md).
This document owns shared terminology, constraints and lifecycle context.

This document records the agreed product direction for future work. Implement
the backlog task by task, refining the relevant entry immediately before its
implementation. IDs are stable references, not delivery priorities or numbered
engineering feature workspaces. No delivery schedule is committed.

Requirements below distinguish confirmed product choices, existing repository
behaviour, and decisions still needed. APIs, schemas, scoring formulas, model
catalogues, migrations, and detailed technical designs are intentionally deferred
until the corresponding task is prepared for implementation. Saving this backlog
does not implement or activate any of its features.

## Product terms

These definitions clarify this backlog; they do not rename existing contracts.

- **Dictionary entry:** a saved source word or phrase, its translation, and its
  associated fields. Existing code and interfaces also call this a dictionary card.
- **Flashcard:** a configurable learning presentation of a dictionary entry.
- **Exercise:** reusable learning content, separate from any learner's answers,
  completion state, or progress. Content may be generated by AI or authored manually.
- **Attempt:** one learner's answer or interaction with an exercise.
- **Practice session:** one run through learning material, with temporary session
  state such as the current item and the items requiring another round.
- **Progress:** a learner's persisted history and learning status, associated with
  the relevant content.
- **Grammar document:** a saved grammar explanation, compact conversation context,
  and associated exercises.

“Stateless exercises” means that reusable exercise content does not carry a
particular learner's completion state. Exercise content is saved and reusable;
attempts and progress are personal and persisted. This requirement does not
prohibit temporary interaction state during a practice session.

## Task files and statuses

The numeric prefix matches the stable BL ID. Keep numbers and slugs stable;
allocate the next unused number for a new task, without renumbering others.
Each task links back here for shared terms, constraints and dependencies.

| Frontmatter status                  | Filename                   | Heading           |
| ----------------------------------- | -------------------------- | ----------------- |
| `pending`, `in-progress`, `blocked` | `NNN-task-name.md`         | Stable task title |
| `done`                              | `NNN-task-name-done.md`    | Ends “— Done”     |
| `skipped`                           | `NNN-task-name-skipped.md` | Ends “— Skipped”  |

Task frontmatter owns status, dates, original task references and evidence paths;
the compact [index](INDEX.md) mirrors status and file links. See the
[metadata contract](../document-metadata.md#specialized-schemas) for fields.
Do not duplicate metadata in a body Status block. Keep detailed requirements,
evidence explanations and lifecycle history in the body.

Done means implemented and verified, not just planned or partially delivered.
Skipped means an explicitly agreed decision not to deliver the task; it is not
a synonym for blocked, failed, deferred or difficult. There are currently no skipped tasks.
Architectural context is not completion evidence merely because an ADR exists.

Agents synchronize task filenames, frontmatter, the compact index and references
as part of backlog delivery, without a separate reminder. The authoritative
completion and skip procedure is
[Backlog synchronization](../../.agent/DELIVERY.md#backlog-synchronization).
This does not authorize implementation, commits, pushes or skipping requirements.

## Shared implementation constraints and sources

Repository facts below were inspected during backlog preparation on 2026-10-01.
Recheck them against current source before starting a task; the repository may
change between backlog preparation and delivery.

- [ADR-0011 — Dictionary persistence and composition](../adr/0011-dictionary-persistence-and-composition.md):
  typed current vocabulary records, immutable revisions, server-owned provenance,
  inherited field settings, private/unlisted sharing, independent forks, and
  explicit references from future learning modules. A generic learning-asset
  payload must not replace the accepted dictionary model. Authorize live
  references at creation and every access; a foreign key or retained share key
  is not permanent cross-owner authority. Archive/revocation makes live content
  unavailable unless a separately authorized snapshot publication exists.
- [ADR-0012 — Dictionary worker and document ingestion](../adr/0012-dictionary-worker-and-document-ingestion.md):
  durable jobs, worker fencing, compatibility, and expand/activate/drain discipline.
  Changing inline acceptance or generation contracts must account for already
  admitted jobs and supported older formats.
- [ADR-0021 — Dictionary AI provider routing](../adr/0021-dictionary-ai-provider-routing.md):
  curated provider/model catalog, immutable job configuration, bounded trusted
  infrastructure adapters, server-held credentials, and stable retry routing.
  Any necessary cross-feature routing decision must be reconciled with this ADR.
- [ADR-0022 — AI credit ledger and generation settlement](../adr/0022-ai-credit-ledger-and-generation-settlement.md):
  reservations, model-specific pricing, settlement/release, and independent
  operational limits. New AI features must integrate with that boundary rather
  than treating access to shared content as the owner's credit entitlement.
  A missing credit account currently means limited access with zero credits;
  this backlog does not establish a free allowance. Existing credit enforcement
  is gated and must not be activated implicitly by a UI change.
- [ADR-0023 — Archived dictionary content deletion](../adr/0023-archived-dictionary-content-deletion.md):
  every future dictionary-owned content store must participate in deletion
  cleanup. Preserve content-free accounting where required without retaining
  deleted vocabulary through exercises or history. Resolve archive, irreversible
  deletion, account removal, and access revocation as distinct cases.
- [ADR-0016 — Runtime UI kit authority](../adr/0016-runtime-ui-kit-authority.md):
  existing shared primitives, semantic tokens, accessibility, localization,
  responsive behaviour, and proportional real-browser/device evidence. New text
  must follow supported locale conventions; direction and language metadata matter.
- [Dictionary Platform](../../.agent/features/021-dictionary-platform/FEATURE.md) and
  its [current guide](../user-flows/dictionary-platform.md): existing field settings,
  lifecycle, authoring, sharing, hidden translation context, and source mappings.
  The current guide and affected source are more current than historical acceptance
  text when later improvements have changed the behaviour.
- [User-flow guides](../user-flows/README.md): create or update applicable guides and
  mapped E2E coverage during implementation, not as if backlog journeys already run.
  Tests use deterministic local AI fixtures and disposable infrastructure;
  they must not invoke paid or nondeterministic models.
- [Root engineering instructions](../../AGENTS.md): application/package boundaries,
  untrusted-input validation, safe rendering/logging, and the applicable delivery
  and verification workflow. Persistence, authorization, model execution, and
  rendering changes require their proportional risk checks during delivery.

## Delivery preparation and dependencies

Each backlog entry is a product grouping. Before implementation, agree its
bounded delivery scope, resolve its open product decisions, then establish
technical design, observable acceptance criteria, and verification. Classify
that delivery under the root workflow; this backlog does not waive feature
authorization or preapprove new architecture, migrations, billing, or deployment.

[BL-003](003-personal-learning-progress.md) supplies durable personal progress for flashcards, sentence practice, and
grammar exercises. [BL-005](005-user-selectable-ai-models.md) supplies the preference required by reader-generated
sentence exercises and future text AI. [BL-007](007-manual-exercise-creation-and-editing.md) builds on the exercise formats
introduced by the relevant practice modes. [BL-008](008-increased-sentence-difficulty.md) and [BL-009](009-practice-plans-for-weak-areas.md) follow baseline
sentence practice. Coordinate these dependencies when selecting a task; do not
present a temporary implementation as satisfying persisted progress or shared
access requirements.

The wide-screen requirements describe the responsive web experience. Native
mobile delivery and precise surface coverage must be agreed for each task;
they are not automatically included by the presence of a mobile workspace.

When a task enters delivery, link its correction/improvement/feature record in
its task file and update its status and this index. Preserve IDs, original task references,
confirmed decisions, and the distinction between future requirements and
completed behaviour.
