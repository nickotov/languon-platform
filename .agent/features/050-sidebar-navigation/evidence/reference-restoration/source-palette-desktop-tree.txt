Present Perfect

Use it for experiences, recent changes and unfinished time periods: “I have visited Paris twice.” “She has just finished the report.”

Past Simple

Use it when the time is finished or stated: “I visited Paris in 2019.” “She finished the report yesterday.”

Signal words

Present Perfect: already, yet, ever, never, since, for. Past Simple: yesterday, ago, last week, in 2019.
