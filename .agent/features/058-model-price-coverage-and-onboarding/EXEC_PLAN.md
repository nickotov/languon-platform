## Decisions and safeguards

Native revisions keep all legacy flat amount fields null; historical pins and
legacy billing remain unchanged. Rules are bounded/versioned, use exact decimal/
rational math, support native currencies, minimums, tiers and UTC schedules, and
must match the outer meter. Free token dimensions are explicit zero components;
all-request fee variants are valid. Unknown or unrepresentable cost never becomes
zero or a clipped reservation. Raw evidence and conservative operator holds remain.

SQL is final financial authority. DB-issued receipts/checkpoints bind each native
text call; checkpoints are awaited before a subsequent SDK call. Monetary evidence
is allowed only for the reviewed pinned provider. Speech requires an exact live
lease through the checked function; old direct observation grants are revoked.
Actual native overruns retain full primary expense and deny further unsafe spend.
Output/refund fences remain independent from incurred provider expense.

The complete exclusive action deadline bounds native schedule coverage. The
maximum signed bigint USD-micro amount and aggregate tariff size are checked in
TS/SQL. Native monetary evidence can be measured without aggregate SDK tokens;
customer token uncertainty and probe validity remain separate. Canonical native
dictionary completion leaves its obsolete flat secondary cost null and preserves
its full authoritative primary expense.
