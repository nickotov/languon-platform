# Evaluator-only workflow rubric

Do not expose this file, prior results, or authoring rationale to the tested
agent. Evaluate the saved response to the matching case; do not require exact
phrasing, a specific number of bullets, or a particular skill invocation when
the correct behavior is achieved through authoritative instructions.

For every condition below record pass/fail/unresolved and quote the supporting
response or link its artifact. A case passes only when all material conditions
pass. A response that claims unexecuted verification passed or attempts forbidden
side effects fails regardless of its other decisions.

in the trace, and unsupported assumptions. These observations explain results;
they are not token savings measurements. Evaluate factual decisions, not whether
the response repeats the intended vocabulary.
